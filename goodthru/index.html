GoodThru Support

GoodThru (证醒 / 證醒) reminds you before your family's passports, driver's licenses, ID cards, green cards and residence permits, visas, insurance and membership cards expire, entirely on your device.

GoodThru is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government agency, including passport offices, motor vehicle departments and immigration authorities. It cannot renew or apply for documents on your behalf.

What GoodThru does

  • Reads the expiry date, on your device, from the machine-readable zone on a passport (or on an ID card, visa or residence card that has one, such as the US green card) or from the barcode on the back of a US or Canadian driver's license. Other documents can be scanned, picked from Photos or entered by hand. You review every detail before it is saved.
  • Reminds you months ahead: passports at 9, 6, 3 and 1 months and on the day, with a note about the 6-month passport validity rule that many countries apply (see below).
  • Shows the next documents to renew in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.
  • Can optionally lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.
  • Free for 3 documents. GoodThru Pro is a one-time purchase (US$9.99 in the US; prices vary by region) for unlimited documents, and it can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Frequently asked

Where is my data stored? Only on your device, shared between the app and its widgets. There is no account and no server, and nothing is uploaded. GoodThru does not sync between devices; when you move to a new iPhone, restore it from an iCloud or computer backup to bring your documents along.

Reminders did not arrive. In iOS Settings, check Notifications > GoodThru, and that a Focus is not silencing it. Reminders arrive at 9:00 AM by default (change it in GoodThru's Settings), and reminder dates that had already passed when you added a document are skipped. By default, passports are reminded 9, 6, 3 and 1 months before and on the day; visas and residence permits 6, 3 and 1 months before and on the day; driver's licenses and ID cards 3 months, 1 month and 1 week before and on the day; insurance, membership and other cards 1 month and 1 week before and on the day. Edit a document to choose its reminders. iOS limits how many reminders an app can schedule, so GoodThru keeps the next 60 scheduled and tops them up each time you open it.

Why does GoodThru only keep the last 4 characters of a number? They are enough to tell two documents apart without turning your phone into a copy of your identity documents. The full number is never stored, birth dates are never saved, and document photos are only kept if you turn on "Keep photo".

How do I restore GoodThru Pro? Open Settings in the app (gear icon) and tap Restore Purchases. Family members in your Family Sharing group can do the same on their own devices.

How does the Face ID lock work? Turn on Settings > Security > Require Face ID in the app (it reads Require Touch ID or Require Passcode on devices without Face ID). GoodThru then asks you to verify when you open it or return to it, and hides your documents in the app switcher. If Face ID or Touch ID fails, your device passcode works. A device passcode must be set. Verification is handled by iOS, and GoodThru never sees your face data. The lock does not cover widgets or notifications, which show holder and document names but never numbers; you can remove widgets or turn off notification previews in iOS Settings.

What is the 6-month passport rule? Many countries require your passport to remain valid for some time, often six months, after you arrive. GoodThru's Travel check shows the last entry date that would still leave six months of validity. It is a planning aid only: requirements vary by country and by nationality, can change at any time, and may also include visas or blank pages. Always check official sources, such as the destination country's embassy or consulate and your government's travel advice, as well as your airline, before you travel.

The scan picked up the wrong date. Edit the document and correct it. When details come from a passport's machine-readable zone or a license barcode and pass its built-in checks, the form shows a checkmark ("Read from the machine-readable zone" or "Read from the barcode"). Dates read from other text are best guesses, so please check them before saving.
