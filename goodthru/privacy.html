GoodThru Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

GoodThru (Chinese name 证醒 / 證醒) is developed by Tiancheng Gao. Contact: terrygao907@gmail.com.

Summary

GoodThru does not collect, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code. It does not connect to any server operated by us, so we never receive your documents or any information about them.

What stays on your device

  • The details you save for each document (its type and title, the holder's name, the issuing country, state or organization, the last 4 characters of its number, its issue and expiry dates, its reminder choices and your notes), along with your settings, are stored only on your device, in a container shared between the app and its widgets.
  • Text and barcode recognition run entirely on your device, using Apple's on-device frameworks and, where available, Apple's on-device language model. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

What GoodThru never stores

  • Full document numbers. Only the last 4 characters are saved. The full number exists only in memory while a scan is being read.
  • Birth dates and other personal details on the document. A passport's machine-readable zone or a license barcode also contains details such as birth date, sex, address and physical description. GoodThru uses only the holder's name, the issuing authority, the issue and expiry dates and the last 4 characters of the number to fill in the form you review, and never saves anything else.
  • Document photos, unless you choose to keep them. "Keep photo" is off by default. When it is off, the image used for recognition is discarded and only the details you review are saved. When it is on, the photo is stored on your device with that document. Turning it off later and saving the document deletes the saved photo.

Face ID lock

The optional lock (Require Face ID, shown as Require Touch ID or Require Passcode on devices without Face ID; your device passcode always works as a fallback) is off by default and is handled entirely by iOS. GoodThru only learns whether verification succeeded. It never sees or stores your face data, fingerprints or passcode.

Widgets and notifications

Widgets and reminders show the holder's name, the document's name and its expiry date or time remaining. They never show document numbers. Widgets and notifications are not covered by the app lock, and Lock Screen widgets and notification previews can be visible without unlocking your iPhone. You can remove widgets or change notification previews in iOS Settings at any time.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a document. The photo picker only gives GoodThru access to the individual images you select. GoodThru never browses your photo library.

Purchases

GoodThru Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store, including Family Sharing. We do not receive your name, payment details or any other personal information from the transaction.

Backups and deletion

GoodThru does not sync your documents through iCloud or any other service. Your data is included in your own device and iCloud backups under Apple's terms. Deleting a document removes it and its reminders from the app; deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Your rights

Because we do not collect any personal data, we hold nothing about you that could be accessed, corrected, exported or deleted. You control all of your data in the app. If you have any question about your privacy, email us at the address above.

Children

GoodThru does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Documents you add for family members, including children, stay only on your device.

Not a government service

GoodThru is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government agency, including passport offices, motor vehicle departments and immigration authorities.
