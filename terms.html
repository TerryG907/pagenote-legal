1. What PageNote does

PageNote imports PDF lecture material, generates study notes for each page using an AI model, and matches an imported problem set against the pages your course has covered. Reading, Apple Pencil annotation and PDF export do not require a subscription or a network connection.

Notes are generated by an AI model and can be wrong. They may misread a diagram, misstate a formula, or invent an explanation that sounds right. Treat them as a study aid, not as an authority.
