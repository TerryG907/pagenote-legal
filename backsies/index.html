Frequently asked

Where is my data stored? Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Reminders did not arrive. Check Settings > Notifications > Backsies, and make sure the receipt's return or warranty date is in the future. Reminders fire at 9:00 AM two days before and on the last return day, and seven days before a warranty ends.

How do I restore Backsies Pro? Open Settings in the app (gear icon) and tap Restore Purchases.

Can I change the default return window? Yes. Settings > Return Policy lets you set days and months per category. Changes apply to receipts you add afterwards.
